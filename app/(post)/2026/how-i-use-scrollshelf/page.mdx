And when I see something, I just hit the save and boom, I have it on scrollshelf web as well — I set up the reminder and when I am working, I can easily get the transcript and summary as well, also can save my notes too.
What I usually do is, I see a new idea and I happen to send it to claude code or codex (using orca btw, and strongly recommend it: [https://www.onorca.dev/](https://www.onorca.dev/))
and I get the ai copy — which is the summary + the transcript + your notes, and give it to my agent as a new worktree. It has been immensely helpful for me, it is easy to use and to the point.
